disscussion about Resident Evil 6
One of the biggest problems of Resident Evil 6 (2012) was that it had too many expectations.

After Resident Evil 5, many players have actually been hoping to see:

* Leon;

* Chris;

* Ada;

* Wesker-related plots;

These characters really intersect.

Capcom did realise this wish in the end.

From the perspective of production, Resident Evil 6 is almost like a "series finale level":

* Lyon faces the presidential incident and the shadow of Raccoon City;

* Chris faces BSAA internal problems and past traumas;

* Ada revealed the conspiracy after the opening;

* Jack inherits the blood of Wesker and connects the old and new eras.

This scale was really exaggerated at that time.

⸻

You said "I respect the idea of some players who want to bring the characters together". I think this evaluation is reasonable.

The player's wish itself is not wrong.

Because for a series that has experienced more than ten years of development, it is indeed a kind of satisfaction to see these characters meet.

But the problem is:

Satisfying feelings does not necessarily mean being suitable for game design.

Resident Evil 6 actually stuffs too many things:

* Horror;

* Action;

* War;

* Two-person cooperation;

* Multi-line narrative;

* Global disaster;

* New and old roles alternate.

As a result, there is content in each direction, but it is difficult to concentrate like Resident Evil 2 Remake.

⸻

However, I also agree with what you said. Many things in Resident Evil 6 are actually not so bad.

Especially some designs:

1. The character selection interface is very powerful.

The character selection interface is actually very "epic".

You see:

* Leon;

* Chris;

* Jake;

* Ada;

There will be a kind of:

"This is the collection of the main characters in the world of Resident Evil."

This feeling.

Nowadays, many games like to choose characters, but the interface of 6 does have the weight of the work of the times.

⸻

2. The action system is actually very mature.

Many people criticise "6" for being too action-oriented, but if you look at the operation alone:

* Sliding shovel;

* Tumbling;

* Close combat system;

* Firearm action;

* Two-person cooperation;

Actually, it's very smooth.

It can even be said that the action base of 6 later influenced a lot of third-person action shooting design.


3. Remaking is a difficult project.

I also agree with this very much.

Many people say:

"Just remake Resident Evil 6."

But in fact, it is much more difficult than Resident Evil 2 and Resident Evil 3.

Because:

Resident Evil 2 Remake

→ Focus on improving a core horror experience.

Resident Evil 4 Remake

→ Improve a mature action horror structure.

But Resident Evil 6 Remake needs to face:

* Four plot lines;

* A large number of scenes;

* Multi-role action system;

* Cooperation mechanism;

* A huge number of performances.

If it's just to improve the picture, it makes no sense.

If it is changed, it may lose the feeling of "crazy large-scale commercial blockbuster" of the original work.

⸻

In fact, I think Resident Evil 6 is a little bit like Ghost Cry 4:

At that time, many people thought there was something wrong with it, but looking back many years later, they would find that:

It's not that it has no advantages, but that it wants to do too many things.

The problem of Ghost Cry 4 is:

"Semi-finished product sense" and process design problems.

The problem of Resident Evil 6 is:

"Ambition goes beyond the most core positioning of the horror series."

But they all have a solid foundation.

And combined with what you said earlier that you like Ghost Cry 5 and Virgil, I think you may be more acceptable to "character-driven" works like Resident Evil 6.

Because the biggest charm of "6" is actually not horror, but:

"These characters who have accompanied players for more than ten years finally stand on the same stage."

If the future is really remade, I hope that Capcom will not completely overturn it, but retain the feeling of "big event" and readjust the rhythm and horror ratio.

Maybe it will be a completely flipped work.
